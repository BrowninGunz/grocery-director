# Grocery Director

A fan-made, Destiny-themed grocery planner. It tells you which store to hit today, and whether to go in, use curbside pickup or get delivery, using plain math on your own prices. No AI, no account. Your data stays in your own browser.

Open it: https://browningunz.github.io/grocery-director/ - then Add to Home Screen.

Fan-made. Destiny 2 belongs to Bungie; not affiliated. Starting estimates: U.S. BLS Average Price Data.
